About the series

Photographs are often treated as proof that a moment happened, but they cannot show everything we felt or understood at the time. As I Remember It explores this concept to show how or memories can change and distort with age or circumstances.

In the childhood images, early 2000s patterns cover faces and interrupt familiar scenes. The patterns recall the nostalgic visuals of my childhood, but they also conceal parts of it. They suggest how a memory can remain vivid in its colours or atmosphere while the people and details become harder to place. In the images representing adolescent years, glitches and colour shifts pull the photograph apart. These disruptions reflect how growing older and becoming more self-conscious can change the feelings associated with a memory.
